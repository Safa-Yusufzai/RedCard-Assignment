RED CARD 2018

China Digital Football Awards

The Red Card is globally recognised as the leading annual report to assess the most influential European football clubs, leagues and players online in China.

Now in its 7th year, Red Card 2018 has been reimagined to include 5 additional awards to recognise the many areas of digital performance in China.

This report aims to educate teams and organisations on how to improve their China digital strategy, what today's Chinese fan wants and what works online in China.
